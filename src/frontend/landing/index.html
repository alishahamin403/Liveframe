Why it works

Built for the moment, not a feed.

Every choice exists to make the night feel alive — not to build another social network.

🚫
No app, no account
It runs entirely in the mobile browser. Guests scan and they're shooting — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for. Friction is what kills participation, so we removed it.
🎞️
Twenty shots, no retakes
The disposable-camera limit changes behaviour. People think before they tap, wait for the real moment, and commit — fewer photos, but far better ones.
⚡
Instant, and safe
Photos hit the wall within seconds after an automatic content check, and the host can pull anything down with one tap. Live energy, without the worry.
📺
A shared big screen
The wall turns private snapshots into a moment the whole room experiences together. That reaction is the thing a camera roll can never give you.
🍁
Canadian by design
Photos and guest data stay on Canadian infrastructure with no cross-border transfers, so venues and hosts meet PIPEDA obligations without guesswork.
